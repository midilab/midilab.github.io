A **DIY multitrack sequencer**, **compose tool**, and **MIDI controller**, designed using readily available, inexpensive parts. It supports different microcontrollers and can be easily assembled on a protoboard or ordered as a PCB.

From barebone to full hands-on control! You can hook up to 16 pots, 24 buttons/LEDs, MIDI interfaces, and CV/Gate to extend your barebone Aciduino.
